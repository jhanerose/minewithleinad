CHAPTER I — GRADE 3
The First Hello
They were classmates. Seatmates. Two small worlds placed side by side in a third-grade classroom.

Jhane was bright and talkative — the kind of person whose energy filled a room. Leinad was quiet, watching from the edges. Then one afternoon, sunlight caught Jhane's face mid-laugh, and Leinad felt something he couldn't name yet.

He remembers lending her his car eraser. She still remembers it too.

That year ended. Leinad transferred schools. The story paused — but it never closed.
💻
CHAPTER II — 11 YEARS ONLINE
Facebook Mutuals & Late-Night Calls
They stayed mutuals on Facebook for nearly eleven years. Not strangers. Not close. Something in between — two people who never fully let go.

Occasionally they'd catch up. A message here. A reaction there. Then gaming sessions began — Mobile Legends matches, chaotic and fun. Late-night Discord calls that stretched past midnight. Pandemic isolation made space for something more honest.

Two people, separated by schools and years, still somehow in orbit of each other.
📚
CHAPTER III — THE BUSY YEARS
Drifting But Never Gone
Life got loud. Jhane poured herself into her studies, building her future with focus and discipline. The schedules never quite aligned.

But Leinad kept asking. "Laro tayo?" became its own kind of language — patient, persistent, warm. Senior high brought another drift. The timing was wrong, again.

Still — they waited. Neither one fully moved on. Some threads are stronger than distance.
⛏️
CHAPTER IV — THE MINECRAFT ARC
Building a World Together
Jhane got curious about Minecraft — but couldn't access it. So she mentioned it. Casually. The way you mention things to someone you trust.

Leinad listened. He bought a Minecraft Realms server. Made them both admins. They played. They built. They talked while placing blocks in silence that felt more like comfort than quiet.

Slowly they realized: they were the only ones logging on. Just them. Every evening. Building a shared world block by block — without ever naming what it was becoming.

But they both knew.
💙
CHAPTER V — JUNE 27, 2024
Finally, In the Same World
They met in person. After all those years — the Grade 3 classroom, the Facebook scrolls, the Discord calls, the Minecraft nights — they stood in the same place at the same time.

A friend of Leinad's came along. But the moment they were together, everything settled. Comfortable. Inevitable. Right.

First love. After years of unfinished timing. After all the loading screens and disconnections and drift — destiny kept bringing them back to the same server.

June 27, 2024. The day the world saved.

TO: JHANE ROSE & LEINAD CLARK

I don't think there's a word for what you two are. "Childhood sweethearts" is too simple. "Reconnected lovers" misses the texture of eleven years of almost. What you are is something rarer — two people who kept existing in each other's periphery, who the universe kept placing back into orbit, who waited without knowing they were waiting.

He lent you an eraser in Grade 3. You still remember it. That's not nothing. That's everything.

Eleven years of Facebook notifications. Late-night Mobile Legends. Discord calls that stretched into morning. A Minecraft Realms server where you were always the only two logged on. You built a world together before you knew you were building a life.

June 27, 2024 was not where the story began. It was where it finally caught up to itself. Where the red string pulled taut after years of slack. Where two people who had been circling finally stopped and stood still and said — oh. It's you. It was always you.

Twenty-three months, Jhane and Leinad. Block by block. Level by level. Every loading screen and disconnection and drift — it brought you here. And here is beautiful.
