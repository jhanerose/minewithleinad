OUR STORY
📖
GRADE 3
CHAPTER 1 — WORLD SEED
Classmates & Seatmates
They were placed side by side in Grade 3 — a cheerful, talkative girl named Jhane Rose and a quiet, shy boy named Leinad Clark. One afternoon, sunlight hit Jhane's face just right as she smiled, and something in Leinad's chest quietly decided: she matters.

He never said it out loud. He just lent her his car eraser — the one she still remembers — and kept it locked away like a secret item in his inventory.
💌 First Crush 🚗 Car Eraser ☀️ Sunlight Smile
GRADE 3
CHAPTER 2 — SERVER DISCONNECT
The Transfer
After Grade 3, Leinad transferred schools. No goodbye. No number exchanged. Just an empty seat where a shy boy used to sit — and a girl who would remember the eraser for years without knowing why it stayed with her.
📦 Server Offline ❌ Disconnected
~11 YRS
CHAPTER 3 — MUTUAL FOLLOW
Facebook Mutuals & Occasional Catch-Ups
They stayed mutuals on Facebook for nearly eleven years. The occasional "how are you" that never went anywhere. Both growing up in their own directions — Jhane focused on her studies, Leinad quietly keeping her on his friends list like a saved game he wasn't ready to delete.

There were gaming sessions, brief online catch-ups, and late-night messages that faded with the morning. The pandemic brought them to Discord. Proximity — digital proximity — that almost became something.
📱 Facebook Mutuals 🎮 Mobile Legends 🌙 Late Night Talks 🎧 Discord Calls
BUSY ERA
CHAPTER 4 — AFK BUT WAITING
Schedules That Kept Getting in the Way
Life got busy. Jhane threw herself into her studies. Leinad kept inviting her to play — "come on, just one game" — but the timing was always off. Senior high school stretched the silence longer.

But neither of them unfriended. Neither fully let go. Two players in the same lobby, just waiting for the other to finally hit ready.
📚 Focused on Studies ⏳ Different Timelines 🔕 Quiet but Present
MINECRAFT
CHAPTER 5 — THE MINECRAFT ARC ⛏️
One Server. Two Admins. A World Built for Two.
Jhane got curious about Minecraft but couldn't afford it. She mentioned it to Leinad. He didn't hesitate — he bought a Minecraft Realms server. Made her admin.

Slowly they realized they were the only ones ever playing on it. No one else logged in. No one else needed to. They were building something without naming it yet — a world that only made sense when both of them were in it.
⛏️ Minecraft Realms 🌍 Co-op World 👑 Both Admins 🏠 Building Together
JUNE 2024
CHAPTER 6 — FIRST MEETING ✨
The Loading Screen Finally Ended
After all those years — Grade 3 to now — they finally met in person. Leinad brought one of his friends. Jhane walked in. And the moment they were in the same room, something settled into place that had been quietly waiting since a sunny classroom afternoon years ago.

It felt like home. Like opening a world that had been paused for years, and finding everything exactly where you left it — just waiting to be resumed.

Dear Jhane Rose,

I don't think there's a word in any language — pixel or real — for what it means to find someone twice. But here we are: Grade 3 and then twenty-three months, with eleven years of almost in between.

I remember the car eraser. I remember thinking you were the brightest thing in that classroom. And then you were gone, and I grew up carrying something I didn't have a name for yet.

We stayed mutuals. We played games. We talked when the timing allowed and went quiet when it didn't. I kept inviting you to play because it was the only excuse I had to keep you close. When you said you couldn't afford Minecraft, I didn't think twice — because that server was never really about the game. It was about finding a world where only you and I showed up.

And we were the only ones who ever did.

Twenty-three months ago, the loading screen finally ended. You walked in and it felt like a world that had been waiting to be opened. Every block we've placed since then — every late night, every quiet call, every game — has been us building something that was always supposed to exist. We just needed the timing to catch up.

Some worlds were always meant to reconnect. I'm glad ours did. I'm glad it's you, every single time.

✦ ᛏ ᛒ ᛖ ᛗ ᛚ ᛜ ᛞ ✦ ᛠ ᛢ ᛤ ✦
— with 💙, always    Leinad
